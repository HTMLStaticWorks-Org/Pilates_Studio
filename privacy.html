PRIVACY NOTICE

Quietly handled.
Never sold.

Effective November 2026 for all digital accounts, waitlist requests and in-studio visits.

FORME Pilates Studio collects only the details needed to manage class bookings, membership billing and optional movement preferences you share with your instructor. We never sell or rent member contact data to third-party advertisers.

In this demonstration build, display theme and layout direction preferences stay locally in your browser rather than on remote tracking servers.

INFORMATION PRACTICES

How your data
is managed.

Transparent safeguards for account details, health notes and newsletter communications.

01 / ACCOUNT DATA

Bookings & contact details

We record your name, email, phone number and class attendance solely to confirm reservations, manage waitlists and communicate schedule changes from the studio.

02 / WELLNESS NOTES

Optional intake context

Any movement goals or injury notes shared with your coach are kept confidential within our instructional team to guide safer spring choices and posture modifications.

03 / PREFERENCES

Browser storage & emails

Local storage saves your preferred color theme and RTL or LTR reading direction. Studio newsletters are opt-in only and include one-click unsubscribe options at any time.

04 / YOUR RIGHTS

Access, updates & removal

You may request a copy, correction or permanent deletion of your studio profile and inactive booking history at any time by contacting reception or emailing our privacy desk.
