SESSION PACING

Inside the
50 minutes.

A predictable arc inside every group session so energy builds steadily without burnout.

00 – 08 MIN

Grounding & Setup

Spring checks, rib-cage breathwork and subtle pelvic alignment cues on light resistance.

08 – 24 MIN

Heat & Activation

Footwork sequences, scapular organization and foundational trunk preparation.

24 – 42 MIN

Loaded Conditioning

Full-body carriages, standing lunges, upper-body pull straps and controlled core blocks.

42 – 50 MIN

Lengthen & Reset

Hip-flexor openers, long stretch progressions and two minutes of quiet floor breath.
