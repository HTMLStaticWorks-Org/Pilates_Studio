MEMBERSHIP & ETIQUETTE

Essential studio
guidelines.

Designed around punctual starts, transparent renewals and thoughtful shared spaces.

01 / RESERVATIONS

Booking & 12-hour notice

Group Reformer and Mat classes can be reserved up to 14 days in advance. Cancellations or reschedules made at least 12 hours prior return class credit to your account automatically.

02 / MEMBERSHIPS

Renewals & pauses

Monthly studio memberships renew automatically on your billing date. Studio Monthly and Unlimited plans may be paused once per calendar quarter with 7 days written notice prior to renewal.

03 / READINESS

Health & grip socks

Clean grip socks are required on all Reformer carriages and studio mats. Please consult an appropriate healthcare professional before joining if you are pregnant, injured or recovering from surgery.

04 / ARRIVAL

Studio doors & check-in

Studio reception opens 15 minutes before class. New visitors should arrive 10 minutes early for spring orientation; arrivals more than 10 minutes past start time may forfeit their carriage.
